Follow the Money. Fight the Fraud.

Cryptocurrency’s decentralized and pseudonymous nature makes it attractive to fraudsters, but it also leaves a digital trail. At Hack Bridge Cybersecurity, we specialize in Fund Tracing and Fraud Investigation to help victims of scams, hacks, and unauthorized transfers track their digital assets and pursue recovery.

How We Help

Blockchain Transaction Analysis

Using advanced blockchain forensics tools, we follow the movement of stolen or misappropriated funds across wallets, exchanges, and mixing services.

🛑 Fraud Identification

Our experts assess the type of fraud — whether it’s phishing, Ponzi schemes, rug pulls, or impersonation — to tailor the investigation process.

⚖️ Collaboration with Exchanges & Authorities

We liaise with global exchanges, regulators, and in some cases law enforcement to freeze illicit transactions and strengthen recovery efforts.

📊 Detailed Investigation Reports

We provide clear, actionable reports on fund flow, wallet addresses, and fraud patterns that can support legal cases or regulatory complaints.

At Hack Bridge Cybersecurity, we don’t just trace funds — we provide the intelligence, strategy, and support you need to fight back against fraud and reclaim your assets.
